Music

A Music library's songs are read by their tags (artist, album, track, year) and grouped into artists and albums, with each album's cover from its files, its folder, or MusicBrainz. Music videos are songs with a picture.

  • Queues: play an album or a song, and it's queued on the screen you choose. Shuffle and repeat are the queue's own.
  • Playlists are yours alone.

Photos

A Photos library holds the photos in its folders, and those uploaded to it. Photos, at the top, is the gallery of every photo library you see. Uploads go into the photo library you choose there; you're given one of your own if you have none.

A caption can be changed by anyone who sees the photo; it's deleted by whoever may change its library. A photo from a folder is only taken out of HazelFlix: the file is kept.

Screen savers

When a screen isn't playing, it shows photos: those of the photo libraries ticked for it in Settings (under Browsers, or Players on the network), of those its profile sees. With none ticked, every photo library it sees. See TVs.
