# HazelFlix help

<!-- no index -->

**HazelFlix is run from your phone.** It isn't a media server you browse on the TV with its remote: your phone is the remote. You find a film on your phone, press Play, choose a screen (the TV in the living room, the projector, a browser on a laptop), and it plays there. The phone then shows what's playing, with pause, skip, the sound and a bar to seek with.

So the idea is: **your films live in one place, your phone chooses, and any screen plays.** The screens can be DLNA TVs and players, which HazelFlix sends video to, or any browser left on *Ready to play*. Each screen sees only what its profile allows, so the kids' TV only ever plays the kids' films, whoever sends them.

It does the rest of what you'd expect too: it finds what your films are (posters, people, ratings), keeps where each person got to, converts files so every screen can play them, and shows your photos when a screen is idle.
