# HazelFlix on your phone

HazelFlix is made to be used from a phone: it's the remote for every screen in the house. Put it on your home screen, and it opens like an app: its own icon, full screen, without the browser's address bar.

![A QR code of HazelFlix's address](qr.svg#right:200 "Point your phone's camera at it")

**The quickest way:** open this page on the computer, and point your phone's camera at the code beside this. It's HazelFlix's address, and your phone offers to open it. (Seen anywhere but in HazelFlix, there's no code: it's made by HazelFlix as you look.)

Or type it: you'll need HazelFlix's address on your network, such as `http://living-room-mac.local:8095` (the computer's name, then `.local`), or its number, such as `http://192.168.1.20:8095`. HazelFlix must be started to listen on the network (`--web 0.0.0.0:8095`, as in [Getting started](getting-started.md#reaching-it-from-your-phone-and-tvs)).

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open **Safari** (it has to be Safari) and go to HazelFlix's address.
2. Press the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen; on an iPad, at the top).
3. Scroll down and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Leave the name as *HazelFlix*, and press **Add**.

Its icon is now on your home screen. Opened from there, it fills the screen, with no Safari around it.

> **Once, after adding it:** an app on the home screen keeps its own sign-in, apart from Safari's, so choose your profile (and type your code) once more the first time you open it.

## Android

1. Open **Chrome** and go to HazelFlix's address.
2. Press the **⋮** menu, at the top right.
3. Choose **Add to Home screen** (on some phones, **Install app**).
4. Choose **Add** (or **Install**), and then *Add* again, or drag the icon where you want it.

> Chrome only opens it as an app of its own (no address bar) when HazelFlix is reached over HTTPS. Over plain `http://` on your network, it's a shortcut that opens HazelFlix in Chrome, which works just the same.

## Using it from your phone

- **Play** on any film asks **Play where?**: this phone, or a screen by name. See [Playing](playing.md).
- **Remote**, at the top, is the screen that's playing: pause, skip, stop, the sound, and a bar to seek with. Your phone can sleep; the film carries on.
- **Photos:** upload from your phone to a photo library, and they're on the screen savers straight away. See [Music and photos](music-and-photos.md).
