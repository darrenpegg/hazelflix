# Playing

## Here, or on another screen

Press Play (or Resume) on a film or episode. When another screen is ready, you're asked **Play where?**: this browser, or a screen by name.

A **screen** is:

- a browser set to *Play* (Settings, Browsers), showing **Ready to play**. Tap it once (browsers need that before they'll play sound), and it waits for a film.
- a DLNA TV or player ticked under *Players on the network* in Settings.

Play where? lists only the screens that can play it: a TV whose profile doesn't see the film isn't offered (see [TVs](tvs.md)). With none left, it plays where you are. On a series, an episode's picture plays it too. A series' episodes show as a list or a grid: *List* and *Grid* beside the season tabs, remembered in this browser for every series.

## The remote

*Remote*, at the top: the screens as tabs, what each is playing, and its controls: pause, skip, stop, the sound, and a bar to seek with. It opens on the screen that's playing, unless you choose another.

## Converting

A browser plays some files as they are. The rest play **live**, made playable as they go, marked *live* on their poster. **Converting** (Settings) makes a copy each browser plays, in the background, so live is only until it's done. Press *Convert* on a film's file to move it to the front.

Each browser and player can be given a **format** (Settings, Browsers and Players): what it's sent, made as it plays and kept if that's ticked. On *Default* it's sent the file if it plays it, else the best copy there is, else a stream.

## Subtitles and sound

A film's subtitles are those beside its file (`Film.en.srt`). Its sound track is the language in Settings, *Audio*, or the one chosen on its Edit page.
