# Editing

Anyone who sees a film can change what HazelFlix says about it. On a film's page, each file has **Edit**.

## Its title and details

Its title, year, tagline and description. What you give is kept over the engines' when they're asked again. **Which film it is** finds it again, by search or by an IMDb or TMDB id, if the engines guessed wrong.

## Pictures from the film

![The frame picker: a box over the film, its shape the picture's](images/frame-picker.png#right:360 "Making a poster from the film")

Under *Its pictures*, **From the film** plays the film itself with:

- ◀ and ▶, a frame back and on,
- Play, and the bar to scrub through it,
- a box over the picture: drag it to move it, drag its corner to size it. Its shape is the picture's: a poster's 2:3, a backdrop's 16:9.

Choose which picture it's for beside **Set**: the poster, the backdrop, or (if the film's in one) its collection's. Set makes it from what's in the box.

A picture can be uploaded instead, and *Back to the engines'* undoes either.

An episode's Edit page does the same for its still and its series' poster.

## People

Change someone's character or place, remove them (*Put back* undoes it), or add someone: one HazelFlix knows, or someone new with a photo.

## Genres and collections

Remove a genre or add one. Choose its **collection**, or make a new one by its name.

### Collections

A collection's page has *Edit this collection*: its name and description, its poster and backdrop, films put in (find them by title) and taken out, and Delete for one you made. Collections are everyone's, but each person sees only the films in libraries they see, and one with none of theirs isn't shown to them at all.

## Taking a file out

A file's **Delete** (for the library's maker, or the owner) takes it out of HazelFlix. The file itself is never deleted, and it stays out even though a scan still finds it.
