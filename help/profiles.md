# Profiles

Each person has a **profile**: their own Continue watching, their own queues and playlists, and the libraries shared with them.

## Who's watching

A browser shows *Who's watching* until a profile is chosen, then remembers it (for a year) until it's changed from the profile circle at the top right. With one profile and no code, it's chosen for you.

## The owner

The first profile is the owner's. It sees every library, and it alone changes Settings, scans, fetches IMDb's datasets and starts or pauses converting.

## Adding and removing profiles

Settings, *Profiles*: type a name and **Add**. **Delete** removes one (never the owner's).

## Codes

A profile can have a code, 4 to 12 digits, set on *Your profile*. Who's watching then asks for it, with a dial pad that goes in by itself at the last digit. Setting or changing a code signs that profile out of every other browser.

- Five wrong in a row, and it waits a minute.
- Forgotten? The owner can **Take its code off** in Settings, Profiles (but can't set another's).

## Hidden profiles

**Hide from Who's watching** (Settings, Profiles) takes a profile off Who's watching, so no one chooses it there. It's for a TV: give it the libraries a TV should play, and give the TV that profile. The whole recipe is in [TVs](tvs.md#a-tv-of-its-own-hidden-profiles).

## Its picture

*Your profile*, Its picture: any photo (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP or GIF), cropped to a square.

## Play history

Off at first. Turn on **Keep play history** in Settings, Play history, and each time anyone plays a film or an episode is kept: who, on which screen, when, and how far they got. **View history** shows a week at a time (each play's picture, title, who, the device it played on, when, and its library), newest first, with buttons for the week before and after, and filters for who and which library. **Clear history** (unfold it first) forgets all of it. Only the owner sees Settings, so only the owner sees the history.
